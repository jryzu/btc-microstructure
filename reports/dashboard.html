Who can actually monetize the order book?

The information is real — book signals predicted 1–5s moves in 13 of 13 hourly blocks of a holdout time-stamped before the data existed, and the perpetual prices it first. Nobody at accessible fees can trade it — not as a taker at any tier tested, and not as a VIP0 maker: the skew experiment’s in-sample gains failed replication on the holdout. The book broadcasts information priced at almost exactly its worth.

Finding 01 · replication

Every book signal replicates — and one candidate failed cleanly

Each candidate was scored by out-of-sample Spearman IC on non-overlapping samples in every 6-hour fold of the development region, then the composite signal faced the holdout once, with parameters frozen a day earlier. Imbalance, the perp-book cross signal, OFI and short momentum all replicate; the flow×imbalance interaction died (50–62% sign consistency) and is reported as a failure.

Finding 02 · cross-venue structure

The perp leads; the basis snaps back in seconds

Perp returns predict future spot returns more strongly than the reverse at every lag to 2s, and the perp book predicts spot at IC ≈ 0.30 — the single strongest new signal found. The basis held a tight −4bps band (negative carry regime) with ~5s mean reversion, closed by the spot leg.

Finding 03 · the economics

Real information, no accessible trade

Taker: the EV rule trades only when the predicted move clears round-trip cost plus a margin selected on walk-forward data — and it never fired. Maker: conservative trade-through fills are the adversely-selected subset by construction; pulling quotes on strong signals removes benign and toxic fills alike, so per-fill losses persist while exposure drops. The optimistic touch model brackets the other side: markouts improve slightly, economics still don’t clear the fee.

Reality check

Where the frictions bind

spread

One tick 99.9% of the time on both venues (~0.001 bps). Crossing it is free; capturing it pays nothing.

fees

The whole story, twice: 3.6–20 bps taker round trips vs ≤1.4 bps of edge; 2 bps maker fee vs 0.007 bps spread capture.

adverse selection

−0.5 to −1 bps markout on conservative maker fills; the priced expression of finding 01, landing on whoever rests at the touch.

latency

Signal half-life ~1s; quotes carry 100 ms simulated latency; local clock offset ~−25ms documented (cross-venue timing shares one clock).

fills

Queue position is unobservable in public data. Through and touch models bracket reality; both were reported, neither flattered.

Fine print

Limitations

  • Two days, one exchange, one (negative-carry) basis regime, no weekend; holdout spans EU+US hours only.
  • Simulated resting orders don’t perturb the queue they join; fill brackets bound but don’t resolve this.
  • Documented data gaps (v1 machine sleep; 2.5h Binance-futures outage on 08-28) masked, never bridged.
  • High ICs measure direction of the next ~tick; magnitudes are fractions of a basis point — the two must be read together.
